# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customer support agents triaging inbound tickets under time pressure. Secondary users are hiring managers reviewing the project as evidence of applied AI engineering.

## Product Purpose

Turn one customer message into a reviewable support decision: intent, urgency, retrieved precedent, grounded draft response, next action, and execution trace. Success means an agent can understand and verify the recommendation without treating the system as an autonomous policy authority.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, operational.

## Anti-references

- Generic chatbot interfaces centered on an open-ended conversation.
- Decorative AI dashboards with invented metrics or opaque reasoning.
- Autonomous-support framing that hides human review and policy limits.
- Dense card grids that fragment one triage workflow into unrelated widgets.

## Design Principles

- Keep the customer message and recommended action visually primary.
- Show evidence beside every consequential recommendation.
- Make degraded, cached, and low-confidence states explicit.
- Keep the seven workflow stages consistent across triage and trace views.
- Prefer familiar support-tool patterns over decorative novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic status text, reduced-motion support, and color-independent severity labels.
